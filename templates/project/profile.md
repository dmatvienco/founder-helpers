# Project profile

The team reads this file at the start of every run. Fill it in — the better it is,
the less your PM has to guess. Bullet points beat prose; keep it current.

## Product

- What is this product, in two sentences?
- Who is it for?
- Where does it run (platforms, store links, URLs)?
- What is the single metric that matters right now (your North Star)?

## Stage and strategy

- What stage are you at (idea / building / launched / growing)?
- What are you focused on this month?
- What is explicitly OUT of scope right now?

## The founder

- How many hours per week do you give this project?
- What do you never want the team to do (taboos, style, tone)?
- Anything the team should know about how you like to communicate?

## Environment

- How is the project built and tested locally?
- Where is production, and how does it deploy?
- Which external services matter (and where do credentials live)?
